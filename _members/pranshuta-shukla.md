Pranshuta Shukla is a second-year Master’s student in Computer Science at the University of Virginia. She completed her Bachelor of Technology with honors in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Jaypee Institute of Information Technology, India. She has also worked as a DevOps Consultant at Oracle for two years, where she focused on production deployments and cloud infrastructure management. Her research interests include computational behavior modeling, AI for healthcare, and AI ethics.
